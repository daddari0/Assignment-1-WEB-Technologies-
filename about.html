About Astana Kindergarden

Astana Kindergarden is a place where children can grow, learn, and explore in a safe and nurturing environment. We focus on holistic development, including cognitive, social, and physical growth. Our experienced educators are dedicated to providing the best early childhood education. We believe that every child is unique and has immense potential.

Key Milestones

  1. 2010: Foundation of Astana Kindergarden.
  2. 2015: Expansion to a second campus.
  3. 2018: Received the National Education Excellence Award.
  4. 2023: Introduced advanced STEM programs for preschoolers.

Quick Facts

  • Over 500 successful alumni.
  • Student-to-teacher ratio is 10:1.
  • Organic, healthy meals prepared daily.
  • State-of-the-art security systems.
